Our Story

The Buttabomma

Born in the heart of Hyderabad, The Buttabomma is more than a boutique — it's a celebration of heritage, craftsmanship, and the timeless beauty of Indian ethnic wear.

Every piece in our collection is thoughtfully curated, blending traditional artistry with contemporary silhouettes. We work directly with master artisans to bring you designs that are as unique as you are.

From hand-embroidered kurtis to opulent occasion wear, each garment tells a story of elegance, grace, and the enduring spirit of womanhood.

— The Buttabomma
